# Courser Meadows: domain language

Courser Meadows is a small grass-based farm at 163 Connors Mill Road, Warner, NH. It sells raw milk, yogurt, eggs, meat, and maple syrup from an on-farm **farm store**, and sells live mini cattle and Idaho Pasture Pigs.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Farm store** | The in-person shop on the farm. The only place things are sold. There is no online store. |
| **Call to reserve** | How customers buy ahead: phone or email to have product boxed and labeled for pickup. Replaces the old cart. |
| **Status bar** | The bar on every page saying whether the farm store is open now. |
| **Override** | The editor forcing the store to show open or closed regardless of the hours. |
| **Closure** | A planned day or range of days the store is shut (holidays). |
| **Guide** | Long-form husbandry writing, e.g. raising Idaho Pasture Pigs. |
| **IPP** | Idaho Pasture Pig: a grazing pig breed the farm raises and sells as feeders and registered breeding stock. |
| **Feeder** | A young pig sold to be raised for meat. |
| **Mini cattle** | Small breeds the farm raises: Mini Jerseys, Lowline Angus, Irish Jerseys (Dexter × Mini Jersey), Dexters. |
| **Herd sire** | A bull used for breeding. |
| **Share** | A whole or half beef bought in bulk, priced by hanging weight. |
| **Wait list** | People waiting for calves, oxen teams, or piglets. Joined by the wait-list forms. |
| **Alprilla** | An outside outlet the farm supplied in 2020. Status unknown. |
