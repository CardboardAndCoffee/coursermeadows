### Meet our herd!

Here at Courser Meadows we raise small breed/miniature cattle (specifically Mini Jerseys, Lowline Angus, and Irish Jerseys) because of their easy handle­ability, sweet dispositions, grazing capabilities, mothering abilities, and their many uses for dairy, beef, and oxen. They are great for homesteaders, small acreage farms, or farms looking for dual/tri-purpose breeds that have great feed conversion. Our cattle range from mature heights of 39-45 inches at the withers and produce anywhere from 1-3 gallons a day of creamy, delicious milk. Our cows are great mothers, easy calvers, and have good fertility. If you are interested in our cattle, email with any questions or to be put on our waiting list. We have breeding, dairy, pet, and beef livestock available throughout the year.

Contact us for more info or to be added to our wait-list.

**All of our milk cows test negative annually for BLV, BVD, and Johne’s disease.**
