**Important Announcement: Changes to Our Store Operations**

Dear Customers,

In 2020, we launched an online store to serve you better during challenging times. We are incredibly thankful for your support and trust in our online venture. However, after careful consideration, we have decided to close our online store at this time.

Rest assured, our in-person farm store will continue to remain open for you to shop and explore our range of products. You can still visit us daily in Warner between 9 AM and 7 PM. Also, always feel free to email ([coursermeadows@gmail.com](mailto:coursermeadows@gmail.com)) or call (603-456-2164) to check on the availability of any product you might be interested in.

Thank you for being a part of our journey, and we hope to see you at our in-person farm store soon.
