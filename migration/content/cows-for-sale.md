Courser Meadows offers calves for sale each year and occasionally has mature cattle available. All cattle are sold halter and lead trained, weaned, and with health testing if old enough. Bred animals will be confirmed by either BioPRYN or palpation by vet. Email us if you have questions or are trying to decide if mini/small breed cattle are for you.

We currently do not ship our cattle with commercial carriers. Any animal(s) bought would need to be picked up by you on our farm or we can usually transport ourselves within New England for a fee.
